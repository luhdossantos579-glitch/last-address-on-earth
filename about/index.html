About Last Address on Earth

Last Address on Earth: how people live in the world's remotest places, from Oymyakon to Tristan da Cunha. One day, one town, how it works.

Who makes this

Last Address on Earth is a YouTube channel from Vision Media. We do the research and write the scripts. The narration is a text-to-speech voice, and we say that in every video's description.

How we work

What we tell you about a place comes from a source we name: published interviews with residents, local government pages, weather records, ferry timetables. Those sources sit in each video's description and at the end of each article. Pictures are public domain, openly licensed or our own; the credits page lists them for every video.
